Pickleball with Friends

Ask to Join

Description

Pickleball is a fun and social paddle sport that combines elements of tennis, badminton, and table tennis. It is easy for beginners to learn and is a great activity to enjoy with friends. Join us for a casual game where all skill levels are welcome.

Cost

Free. Paddles and pickleballs will be provided.

When

Saturday at 10:00 a.m.

Where

Meet at the pickleball courts in Provo, Utah.

What to Bring

Wear comfortable athletic shoes and bring water. You may also bring your own paddle if you have one.
